Questions before you explore

Good to know.

When are card tips paid?

Every Monday, directly to staff bank accounts. Source: case README, The company.

Who sets the split rule?

The restaurant owner sets the rule in the owner dashboard. Source: case README, The company.

Is the staff demo a live financial service?

No. This is a fictional teaching-case alpha. The simulator and concern reporting are demonstrations, not live transactions or secure reporting services.

Can I register a real restaurant here?

No. The form below demonstrates the registration journey only; it does not send or store your information.
